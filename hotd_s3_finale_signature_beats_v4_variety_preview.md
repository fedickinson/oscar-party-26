# House of the Dragon S3 Finale Draft
## Signature Beats v4 — Variety Pass

**Rule:** Draft a character, then activate exactly **3** of their available Signature Beats before the episode.

- Central characters: **7 choices**
- Major supporting characters: **6 choices**
- Support / sleeper characters: **5 choices**
- Every possible 3-beat selection remains **60–90 base points**.
- Opposing bets may both be activated as a deliberate hedge.
