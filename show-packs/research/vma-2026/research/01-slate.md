## CRITICAL METHODOLOGY WARNING - READ BEFORE USING THIS PACKET

**WebFetch was unavailable for this entire research session.** Every external domain returned
`EGRESS_BLOCKED` from the organization's network egress proxy, including all of the preferred
sources: mtv.com, vote.mtv.com, paramountpressexpress.com, billboard.com, variety.com,
deadline.com, rollingstone.com, en.wikipedia.org, and mirrors (thefutoncritic.com, eonline.com,
goldderby.com, tvinsider.com). Per `/root/.ccr/README.md`, a proxy egress denial is an
organization policy decision and must be reported rather than worked around, so no attempt was
made to route around it.

**Consequence: every nominee in this packet is drawn from WebSearch result summaries, not from
the source pages themselves.** That matters in three specific ways:

1. **Category wording is NOT verbatim.** The task asked for "MTV's exact category wording." That
   cannot be delivered without reading mtv.com or the CBS press release. Category names below are
   the names as they appeared in trade-press summaries, which normalize capitalization and
   sometimes shorten ("Best Long Form Video" vs "Best Long-Form Video"). Treat all category
   strings as approximate pending a read of vote.mtv.com.
2. **The search summarizer demonstrably conflated years.** A query scoped to 2026 returned the
   2023 Best Afrobeats nominee list (Rema and Selena Gomez "Calm Down", Ayra Starr "Rush",
   Wizkid "2 Sugar") as though it were current. That list was discarded. Other year-bleed may
   have survived undetected.
3. **Nominee counts may be truncated.** Where a list looks short relative to its peers it is
   flagged inline.

**Nothing in this packet was invented.** Every nominee listed was returned by a search against a
named outlet. But "returned by a search summary" is a weaker evidentiary standard than "read on
the source page," and the whole packet should be re-verified against vote.mtv.com before it is
used to build a live game. See "Verification queue" at the end.

**Screen vs. record distinction (carried throughout):** the CBS broadcast is the *screen*. A
category whose winner is posted to MTV's X or Instagram account after the telecast is an official
result but not a screen event. Part B tracks this distinction; Part C predicts it for 2026.

### Category-existence dispute (important, and resolved)

Billboard's August analysis noted that several 2025 categories were absent from the August 18
announcement: Best Rock, Best Afrobeats, Best Pop Artist, Push Performance of the Year, Best
Album, Video for Good, Best Long Form Video, Best Group and Song of Summer. Some outlets
reported these as "quietly retired."

That framing was **premature for four of them**. The September 18 social-categories announcement
confirms Best Group, Best Long Form Video, Best Album and Song of Summer all exist for 2026 as
social categories. Any source claiming those four were dropped predates September 18 and is
stale.

Genuinely dropped for 2026 (multiple dedicated trade stories, MED confidence):

- **Best Afrobeats** - dropped after three years (2023-2025). Sources: news24.com, bellanaija.com,
  notjustok.com, dancehallmag.com, all dated August 2026.
- **Best Rock** - dropped; reported as the first year without a rock category since 1989.

Status unresolved - **UNCONFIRMED**:

- **Best Pop Artist** - absent from the August announcement; no recovery announcement found. Note
  that "Best Pop" (video) clearly exists, so 2026 may simply have dropped the artist-level tier.
- **Push Performance of the Year** - absent; no recovery announcement found.
- **Video for Good** - absent; no nominee list recovered; not among the four social categories.

### Predicted 2026 on-air set (inference)

Reasoning. The 2026 broadcast is two hours on CBS, the same shape as 2025. In 2025, thirteen of
roughly twenty-six categories never reached the screen. A two-hour network telecast carrying
Snoop Dogg as host, a Nirvana Vanguard tribute, and a Madonna return performance has *less*
award time than 2025, not more. So the 2026 on-air set should be at or below 2025's size.

- Michael Jackson Video Vanguard Award (Nirvana) - a tribute segment, structurally guaranteed

- Best Pop

Predicted NOT on air - posted to social after the telecast (moderate confidence):

- All six craft categories: Best Direction, Best Art Direction, Best Cinematography, Best

Genuinely uncertain: Best R&B, Best Country, Best Alternative, Best Dance, Song of Summer.

compatible with either an on-air reveal or a post-show post.

### The screen-vs-record consequence

For a game modelled on the broadcast, the 2025 precedent implies **roughly half of the 2026
categories will never be a screen event.** A design that assumes every category produces a
watchable on-air moment will misfire badly. The safe architecture treats "official result" and
"screen event" as two separate fields, resolves craft categories as record-only by default, and
keeps a post-show ingestion path for the social dump that in 2025 landed about 25 minutes after
the credits.
