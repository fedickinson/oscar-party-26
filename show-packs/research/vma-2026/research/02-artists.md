## Research conditions and limits — read before using this packet

This pass was run with two hard constraints that shaped the result:

1. **WebFetch was unavailable.** Every direct page fetch was refused by the network egress
   proxy (`EGRESS_BLOCKED`) — billboard.com, rollingstone.com, deadline.com, mtv.com,
   vote.mtv.com, paramountpressexpress.com, en.wikipedia.org, goldderby.com, tvinsider.com,
   soompi.com, eonline.com all refused. `curl` through the proxy returned
   `CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403`. No primary document was read end to end.
2. **The session web-search budget ran out** at 200/200 calls partway through the artist
   sweep. Roughly 20 of 88 artists received a dedicated source check before the budget closed.

Consequence: the **category map and nominee list below are solid** (cross-checked across
Billboard, Variety, Rolling Stone, Deadline, CBS News, NME, Hollywood Reporter, Holler,
Soompi and Korea Herald search results). The **artist dossiers are complete for the tier-A
nominees and are stubs for the long tail**, where only the sourced nomination line is
recorded. Stub sections carry a `THIN SOURCING` banner and deliberately contain no career
facts, no VMA history and no 2026 status, because none were verified. They are omissions,
not gaps to be filled by guessing. A second pass with web access restored would complete them.
